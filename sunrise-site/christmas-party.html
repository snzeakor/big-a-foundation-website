FIFTEEN YEARS

A Dallas holiday tradition.

For the past fifteen years, Big A Foundation has hosted a Children's Christmas Party for local Dallas youth, with toys and food for every family who comes through the door. Every child gets a free gift, with face painting, games and music to go with it.
