Partner with Big A.

From the Children's Christmas Party to AFRIMMA Fest, our events reach thousands of families across Dallas-Fort Worth and across Africa. Sponsors and partners help make that possible.

Ways to partner.

  • Event sponsorship for the Children's Christmas Party, AFRIMMA Fest, and new arts and culture programming like Comedy Across Continents.
  • In-kind support: toys, food, school supplies, venue space, printing, or professional services.
  • Partnership on programs serving DFW families or communities in Nigeria and Africa.

No pricing or sponsorship tiers are listed here. Reach out and we will put together options that fit.
